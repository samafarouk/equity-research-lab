# Equity Research Report: Arabian Cement Company (ARCC.CA)
Last Updated: September 2026

## Executive Summary

This repository contains the comprehensive equity research coverage and financial valuation of **Arabian Cement Company (ARCC.CA)**, prepared as part of the **CFA Institute Research Challenge**.

We initiate coverage on ARCC with a **BUY** recommendation and a **12-month target price of EGP 124 per share**, representing a **69% upside** from the current share price of EGP 73.45.

Our investment thesis centers on ARCC's earnings recovery through improving utilization, cost efficiency, pricing normalization, and export flexibility. The analysis combines bottom-up financial modelling, industry analysis, DCF valuation, relative valuation, sensitivity analysis, and an ESG integration scenario.

### 1. Volume Recovery & Operating Leverage

ARCC's earnings recovery is supported by higher plant utilization and improving cement demand. With installed capacity held at approximately **5 Mt**, forecast sales volume increases from **4.85 Mt in 2025A to 5.00 Mt by 2030F**. Higher utilization supports incremental volume and greater absorption of fixed operating costs, contributing to margin expansion.

### 2. Cost Efficiency & Margin Expansion

The forecast incorporates a sustained improvement in profitability following the 2025 earnings inflection. Revenue growth is predominantly pricing-led, with total ASP increasing from **EGP 2,565/ton in 2025A to EGP 3,861/ton by 2030F (+50.5%)**, while total sales volume grows by approximately 3%.

A detailed COGS bridge and cost-driver analysis was developed to assess raw materials, transportation, overhead, production volume, and COGS per ton. Gross margin remains elevated through the forecast period as pricing growth and cost efficiency outpace unit-cost inflation.

### 3. Export Platform & FX Diversification

ARCC's strategic Suez location and established international presence provide flexibility beyond the domestic market. Export exposure diversifies demand, provides FX-linked revenue streams, and supports capacity utilization during periods of domestic market weakness.

### 4. ESG as Financial Optionality

ESG was analyzed through both a qualitative scorecard and a financial integration scenario. Energy efficiency and alternative-fuel adoption were translated into potential fuel-cost savings, lower CBAM exposure, transition CAPEX, incremental free cash flow, and an illustrative valuation impact.

## Valuation Summary

Our valuation combines a **70% Discounted Cash Flow (DCF)** weighting with a **30% Relative Valuation** weighting.

- **DCF Fair Value:** EGP 128.5 / share
- **P/E Fair Value:** EGP 113 / share
- **EV/EBITDA Fair Value:** EGP 114 / share
- **Weighted 12-Month Target Price:** **EGP 124 / share**
- **Current Share Price:** EGP 73.45 / share
- **Implied Upside:** **69%**

### DCF Framework

The DCF forecasts FCFF from 2026F–2030F and discounts the resulting cash flows using a declining WACC profile as the assumed macroeconomic environment normalizes.

- **WACC:** 24% in 2026F → 17% in 2030F
- **Terminal Growth Rate:** 5%
- **FCFF:** EGP 4.11bn in 2026F → EGP 6.11bn in 2030F
- **DCF Fair Value:** EGP 128.5/share
